      About

      A watch-only model of downtown Greensboro, NC. Nothing here is faked: windows light up where something is open or on, crowds gather only at real events, and the sign on the Lincoln Financial tower shows the real time and temperature. Items come from public posts gathered into a digest every 30 minutes; posts by private individuals show no names, and each item links to its original post. Crime is reported in the paper only, never on the map.

      The first citywide sketch map is still up.

Facades: Mapillary street photos, © Mapillary contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0. Weather: National Weather Service. Roads: NCDOT DriveNC. Restaurants and bars: OpenStreetMap; chains left out.
